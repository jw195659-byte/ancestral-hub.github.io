Privacy & Security

Family history is personal. We built the whole product around that.

Most genealogy platforms make their money from public trees and shared data. Ancestral Hub doesn't — your membership fee is the entire business, which means there's no incentive to make any part of your history visible to anyone but you.

The vault

What "private" actually means here

01 — ENCRYPTION

Encrypted at rest and in transit

Every document, tree entry, and note is encrypted in storage and while moving between your device and ours.

02 — VISIBILITY

No public profiles, ever

There is no public version of your tree. It cannot be indexed by search engines or browsed by non-members, by design.

03 — SHARING

You choose who sees what

On Family and Legacy plans, sharing is invite-only, relative by relative, and revocable at any time.

04 — OWNERSHIP

Your data leaves when you do

Export your full tree and documents whenever you like. Delete your account and the underlying data is removed, not archived.

Our commitments

What we will never do

  • →

    We will never sell or license your data

    Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to other genealogy companies. The membership fee is how this business is funded.

  • →

    We will never publish your tree without explicit action from you

    There's no default "public" setting to opt out of — public visibility doesn't exist as a feature.

  • →

    We will never show you ads

    No third-party ad networks, no tracking pixels from advertisers, no sponsored "record matches."

  • →

    We will never lock your data in

    Full export is available on every plan, in standard formats, at no extra charge.
