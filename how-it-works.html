Process

From a name to a documented tree, in five steps.

Every step happens inside your private account. Nothing you enter is visible to other members, and nothing is indexed publicly — not your tree, not your documents, not your search history.

01

Start with what you know

Enter the names, approximate dates, and places you already have. Even one grandparent's name and hometown is enough of a starting thread.

02

We search the archives for you

Our researchers and record-matching tools comb census data, immigration records, parish registers, and military files for entries that match your thread — privately, under your account only.

03

You confirm each match

Nothing gets added automatically. You review each candidate record side-by-side with what you already know, and approve or reject it.

04

Your tree grows, one proven branch at a time

Confirmed records become tree entries, each one linked back to its source document, so you can always see exactly why a branch is there.

05

Read the story, not just the chart

We assemble the context around the facts — migration routes, historical events, the towns your family left — into a private narrative view of your tree.
