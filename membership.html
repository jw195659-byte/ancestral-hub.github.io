Questions

Before you start

Can anyone else see my tree?

No. Trees are private by default and stay that way. The only exception is the Family and Legacy plans, where you can choose to invite specific relatives — and you can revoke that access at any time.

Is my data sold or shared with advertisers?

Never. We don't run ads and we don't sell member data. The membership fee is the entire business model — see our privacy page for the full commitment.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings with no notice period. You can export your tree and documents before or after cancelling.

What happens to my documents if I downgrade?

If you're over the new plan's storage limit, we'll ask you to remove or export files before the downgrade takes effect — nothing is deleted automatically.
