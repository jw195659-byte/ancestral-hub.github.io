About Ancestral Hub

We built the archive we couldn't find.

Ancestral Hub started with a filing cabinet, not a pitch deck. Our founder spent three years piecing together a family's migration from a single water-stained photograph and a surname spelled four different ways across five countries.

What was missing wasn't more records — it was a place to hold them that didn't ask her to make her family's history public in exchange for access. Every free genealogy tool she tried came with the same trade: search our archive, and your tree becomes part of it too.

So we built something else: a research room that runs on membership fees instead of visibility. No public trees. No incentive to grow a database off the back of your family's records. Just a private, well-organized place to do the work.

Today, Ancestral Hub is a small team of researchers, archivists, and engineers who treat every account the way our founder wished hers had been treated from the start — as a private study, not a public listing.

Est. 2021 Independently owned. No outside data partnerships.
What we hold ourselves to

Three commitments behind everything we build

VALUE — 01

Privacy is the default, not a setting

We don't add public visibility later as a "feature." It was never on the table.

VALUE — 02

Sourced, not guessed

Every entry in your tree traces back to a document. We'd rather show fewer results than unreliable ones.

VALUE — 03

You can always leave with your work

No lock-in. Your tree and documents are yours to export, in full, at any time.
